The Admin product editor now supports:
- Farashin guda (unit price)
- Farashin carton
- Yawan guda a carton
- Discount price
- Stock
- Photo
- Girma / Size (for clothes)

The entered prices are kept exactly as supplied. Carton prices are intentionally left editable/blank until the correct carton price is confirmed.

## Clothing / Talla product fields

For each clothing or other catalog item, Admin > Products supports:
- Main photo
- Front photo
- Back photo
- Colors
- Size
- Quality/material description
- Unit price
- Carton price and carton quantity where applicable
- Stock and visibility
- Video URL for a product demonstration
- Featured/Talla flag so the item appears in the public Talla / Sabbin Kaya section

Prices are editable at any time. The Products list also has quick **Farashi** and **Carton** buttons for fast price changes.
